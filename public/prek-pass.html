❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Pre-K Pass? +
The Pre-K Pass is FREE for kids ages 3–5 and includes unlimited visits all season in 2026 to 22 participating parks. Not valid for park buyouts or separately ticketed events.
Who is eligible? +
Children ages 3–5. Kids 2 and under are already free and do not need a Pre-K Pass.
How do I register? +
Register your child ONLINE by May 31, 2026. You CANNOT register at the park — online only.
When and where do I activate? +
Activation must be completed by May 31, 2026, in person at the park where the Pre-K Pass was registered, at Guest Services. The child must be present.
What do I need to bring to activate? +
Bring your child AND proof of age (birth certificate or passport – original or copy accepted). Also bring your order confirmation and photo ID for the adult pass.
What if I miss the deadline? +
If not activated by May 31, 2026, the Pre-K Pass will not be valid for admission. It cannot be activated after the deadline — no exceptions.
Do I need an adult pass to register? +
Yes. A valid 2026 Silver, Gold, or Prestige Season Pass or Membership is required to register a Pre-K Pass.
What if my child turns 6 during the season? +
As long as you activate BEFORE your child turns 6, the pass remains valid for all of 2026.
Can I add dining or drink plans? +
Yes. All-Season add-ons may be added to the Pre-K Pass, but they must be purchased separately — nothing is included for free.
Does it include parking? +
No. The Pre-K Pass is not valid for parking, discounts, Bring-A-Friend Tickets, or other season pass benefits.
Do supervising adults need park admission? +
Yes. Supervising adults must have valid park admission (Silver, Gold, or Prestige Pass). Children cannot be dropped off or supervised by minors.
