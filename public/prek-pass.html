❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an adult pass to register? +
Yes. A valid 2026 Silver, Gold, or Prestige Season Pass or Membership is required to register a Pre-K Pass.
Who is eligible? +
Children ages 3–5. Kids 2 and under are already free and do not need a Pre-K Pass.
What if my child turns 6 mid-season? +
As long as you activate BEFORE your child turns 6, the pass remains valid for all of 2026.
What do I bring to activate? +
Bring your child AND proof of age (birth certificate or passport – original or copy accepted).
What parks don't accept it? +
Kings Island, Knott's Berry Farm, Six Flags Magic Mountain, Six Flags Discovery Kingdom, La Ronde, Schlitterbahn New Braunfels, Six Flags México, Hurricane Harbor Concord, Los Angeles, Phoenix.
