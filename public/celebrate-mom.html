A note before we begin

Somewhere right now, a mother is folding laundry at midnight after a full day of everything. Somewhere a grandmother is looking at a photo on her phone — a child's face, a birthday party, a sunset — and smiling quietly to herself. Somewhere a mother is sitting with a cup of tea going cold, not because she forgot it, but because she got up three times to help someone else before she could take a sip.

She doesn't ask for much. She never has. But if you've ever watched her pause at a travel magazine, close a browser tab with a sigh, or say "one day we should..." — you already know what she's been dreaming about.

This is your sign. Not just on Mother's Day. Any day. Every day is someone's reason to celebrate their mother. Today might be yours.
