Best Time to Visit by Destination

🇿🇦 South Africa
Best: May–September (dry season). Wildlife congregates around water sources.
🇰🇪 Kenya & Tanzania
Best: July–October (Great Migration river crossings).
🇧🇼 Botswana
Best: May–October (dry season). The Okavango Delta floods June–August.
🦁 General Tips
Avoid March–May (heavy rains). January–February is calving season — excellent predator action.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a luxury safari cost?
Luxury safaris typically range from $5,000 to $15,000+ per person depending on destination, length, and lodge selection. Go2Africa provides custom quotes based on your preferences.
Is a safari safe for children?
Yes! Many lodges welcome families and offer special children's activities. South Africa is particularly family-friendly. Go2Africa can recommend family-specific lodges.
What's included in an all-inclusive safari?
Typically: accommodation, all meals, select drinks, twice-daily game drives, park fees, and internal flights or transfers.
Do I need vaccinations for Africa?
Yellow fever vaccine is required for some countries. Malaria prevention is recommended for most safari destinations. Consult your doctor 4–6 weeks before travel.
