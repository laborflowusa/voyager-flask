From Someone Who Lives Here
7 Things Locals Know That Tourists Don't
01
Arrive at 9:45am
Gates open at 10. Being in line before they open means you get 90 minutes of near-empty park. Hit Steel Force and Hydra back to back while everyone else is still parking.
02
Tuesday or Wednesday
Weekend crowds are brutal in July and August. Midweek visits — especially Tuesday and Wednesday — see significantly shorter queues across every major ride.
03
Bring a Refillable Bottle
You can't bring outside food, but refillable water bottles are allowed. Filling stations are throughout the park. In July heat this will save you $20 in bottled water purchases.
04
Route 222 North Exit
Take the Dorney Park Road exit from 222 North for faster parking lot access. Coming from Allentown downtown — it's a 10-minute drive, not 30. People overcomplicate the drive.
05
Six Flags Pass Hack
Dorney is part of the Six Flags network. A Six Flags Gold Pass gets you into Dorney plus Great Adventure in NJ. If you're visiting twice this season, the pass pays for itself.
06
Hit Water Park at 3pm
Most coaster riders head to Wildwater Kingdom in the morning when it's cooler. By 3pm the water park thins out. Flip the script — do coasters first, water park after lunch.
07
Locker Near Hydra
Secure loose items before the major coasters — policy has gotten stricter. Rent a locker near Hydra or Steel Force. $10–15 for the day and it covers everything in that zone.

Common Questions
Answered Straight
When does Dorney Park open for 2026?
Dorney Park opens Saturday, May 8, 2026. It runs weekends only through Memorial Day weekend, then switches to daily operation from mid-June through Labor Day. Wildwater Kingdom opens May 23. The season wraps up September 7, after which the park runs Halloween-themed weekends.
Does one ticket cover both Dorney Park and Wildwater Kingdom?
Yes. A standard admission ticket gives you access to both parks on the same day. You can go back and forth between the coaster side and the water park as many times as you want. This is what makes Dorney one of the best-value parks in the region.
What's the cheapest way to buy tickets?
Buy online in advance — gate prices run $79.99+ per adult. Online tickets through GetYourGuide can save you significantly. If you're planning two or more visits, a Six Flags Gold Pass (~$105/season) is the best value available and includes Dorney Park plus Great Adventure in NJ and other East Coast parks.
Can I bring food into the park?
Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside the park. You can bring a reusable sealed water bottle — there are filling stations throughout the park. Lockers are available near the front entrance if you want to store items in your car and retrieve them for a mid-day break in the parking lot.
Which coaster should I ride first?
Steel Force. It's the longest steel coaster on the East Coast and the wait balloons after 11am. Get there right when the park opens, head straight to Steel Force, then hit Hydra and Talon while the crowd is still settling in. Save Possessed and Thunderhawk for the afternoon when the main coaster lines peak.
Is there a Six Flags pass that covers Dorney Park?
Yes. Dorney Park is now part of the Six Flags family. A Six Flags Gold Pass or higher includes Dorney Park plus access to other Six Flags properties like Great Adventure in New Jersey and Kings Dominion. For anyone planning multiple park days this summer, this is worth running the math on.
What's the height requirement for the major coasters?
Steel Force and Hydra require 54". Talon requires 54". Possessed requires 54". Thunderhawk requires 48". Most kiddie rides in Planet Snoopy are designed for children under 48". Wildwater Kingdom slides vary — most require at least 42–48" for the major slides.
