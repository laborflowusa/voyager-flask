The Best Lines
Top Cruise Lines for Romance

Not all cruises are created equal for couples. Some specialize in adults-only experiences, others in luxury, and some in adventure. Here's who does what best.

🎸❤️
Virgin Voyages
Adults-Only · 18+ Only
The only major cruise line that's completely adults-only (18+). No kids, no buffets, no upselling. Over 20 included restaurants, incredible nightlife, and the most romantic vibe at sea.
  • 100% adults-only — guaranteed no children
  • All specialty dining included in fare
  • Rooftop sunset bars and private hot tubs
  • Best for: Young couples, honeymooners, anniversary trips
✨🥂
Celebrity Cruises
Adults-Only Solarium · Luxury
Modern luxury with an exclusive adults-only solarium — a glass-enclosed pool and lounge area for couples. Award-winning restaurants, spa experiences, and sophisticated nightlife.
  • The Retreat — exclusive suite-only area with private lounge
  • Adults-only Solarium pool and hot tubs
  • Couples spa treatments and thermal suites
  • Best for: Couples seeking balance of romance and activity
⛴️🌊
Viking Cruises
Adults-Only · River & Ocean
Adult-focused ocean and river cruises (18+). Smaller ships, cultural enrichment, and truly all-inclusive pricing. Perfect for couples who want to explore Europe, Asia, or the Americas together.
  • Completely adults-only — no children under 18
  • All-inclusive — excursions, wine, beer, and soda included
  • Intimate ships (less than 1,000 passengers)
  • Best for: Cultural immersion, river cruises, mature couples
🛳️💎
Regent Seven Seas
Ultra-Luxury · All-Inclusive
The most all-inclusive luxury cruise line. Every suite includes a balcony, all drinks are included, all excursions are included, even business class flights. The ultimate splurge for a milestone celebration.
  • Truly all-inclusive — absolutely everything is covered
  • Largest standard suites in the industry
  • Personal butler service in every suite
  • Best for: Anniversaries, luxury seekers, no-budget trips
🏔️❤️
Princess Cruises
Romantic · Value
The "Love Boat" line. Princess is famous for romance — couples massages, champagne balconies, and the most romantic private island in the Caribbean. Excellent value for couples on a budget.
  • Princess Cays — private island with cabanas for two
  • Ultimate Balcony Dining — private dinner on your balcony
  • Couples Lotus Spa packages
  • Best for: Budget-conscious couples, first-time cruisers
🍝🇮🇹
Oceania Cruises
Cuisine-Focused · Intimate
Finest cuisine at sea. Gourmet restaurants, cooking classes, and food-focused shore excursions. Small ships (under 1,200 passengers) create an intimate, romantic atmosphere.
  • Gourmet dining — rated best food in cruising
  • Small ships, no crowds, personal service
  • Cooking classes and culinary shore excursions
  • Best for: Foodie couples, intimate celebrations

Questions Answered
Frequently Asked Questions
What's the best adults-only cruise line?
Virgin Voyages is the only major cruise line that's 100% adults-only (18+). Celebrity has the best adults-only solarium area. Viking is also adults-only (18+) and excellent for river cruises. For a completely child-free experience, Virgin is your best bet — no kids, no buffets, no upselling.
Which cruise line is best for a honeymoon?
For young couples: Virgin Voyages (nightlife, trendy, adults-only). For luxury: Regent Seven Seas (all-inclusive, butler service). For value: Celebrity (romantic solarium, excellent dining). For a truly unique experience: Paul Gauguin in French Polynesia (overwater bungalows, turquoise lagoons). All offer honeymoon packages with champagne, flowers, and romantic dinners.
How can I make our cruise more romantic?
Book a balcony stateroom — watching sunsets together from your private balcony is worth the upgrade. Purchase the romance package (champagne, strawberries, flowers). Book a couples massage in the spa. Make a reservation at a specialty restaurant for one night. Book the "Ultimate Balcony Dining" experience — private dinner on your balcony. And book formal portraits at sunset — they become cherished keepsakes.
What's included in a couples cruise?
All lines include your stateroom, all meals in the main dining room and buffet, basic beverages (water, coffee, tea, juice), onboard entertainment, pools and hot tubs, and fitness center access. Premium lines like Regent and Viking include all excursions, drinks, specialty dining, and even flights. Romance packages are usually an add-on for $100-200.
Are there cruises specifically for couples?
While no line is exclusively for couples, several cater strongly to them. Virgin Voyages is adults-only and designed for couples and singles. Viking is adults-only with a sophisticated, quiet atmosphere perfect for couples. Celebrity's adults-only solarium is the most romantic public space at sea. Princess is called "The Love Boat" for a reason — they pioneered romantic cruising.
